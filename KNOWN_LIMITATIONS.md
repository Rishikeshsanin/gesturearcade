# Known limitations

Two experiences only, not 20 games. Lab lacks structured interactive test scenarios, lighting analysis, calibration and reliable full confidence. No keyboard gameplay yet, limited mouse fallback. Main-thread inference; CDN model package not yet verified at runtime. No physical capture latency, model accuracy or FPS measurements. AirSlice uses latest physics step rather than timestamp-interpolated fruit positions; false/missed hits possible. Current heuristic can reject legitimate fast motion or accept landmark jumps. No mobile/browser matrix or production acceptance.
